## Who does Spotflow Serve?

- Backend and frontend developers building payment-enabled web or mobile apps
- Finance and operations teams who need automated settlement and reconciliation
- SaaS and e-commerce businesses that require subscription billing and refunds


## The problem it solves

Many businesses struggle with integrating multiple payment providers, handling cross-currency conversions, and automating settlements. 
Spotflow centralizes payment orchestration, reduces engineering effort with clear APIs, and improves reconciliation visibility for operations teams.
